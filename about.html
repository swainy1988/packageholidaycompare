Making Package Holiday Comparison Simpler

PackageHolidayCompare helps travellers compare package holiday offers from different holiday suppliers in one place before choosing where to book.

What is PackageHolidayCompare?

PackageHolidayCompare is a holiday comparison website designed to make it easier to search and compare package holiday offers.

Instead of checking different holiday websites individually, travellers can search by departure airport, destination, travel dates, party size, board basis and budget and compare available offers in one place.

Our goal is to give travellers a simple and transparent way to compare their options before continuing to the chosen holiday supplier to complete a booking.

How it works

1

Search

Enter the details of the holiday you are looking for, including airport, destination, dates and budget.

2

Compare

Review matching hotels and compare available package holiday offers from participating suppliers.

3

Book with the supplier

When you find an offer you like, continue to the supplier's website to check the final details and complete your booking directly with them.

What we aim to provide

✓
Simple comparison

Holiday offers presented in a clear format so travellers can compare important details more easily.

✓
Total holiday prices

Where available, we display the total package price supplied for the holiday offer.

✓
Multiple suppliers

Our comparison service is designed to show offers from different participating holiday suppliers.

✓
Direct supplier booking

PackageHolidayCompare does not take the holiday payment. Bookings are completed with the relevant supplier.

Important comparison information

PackageHolidayCompare is a comparison service rather than a travel agent or holiday supplier.

  • We do not take payment for package holidays displayed on the website.
  • Holiday bookings are completed directly with the supplier you choose.
  • Prices and availability can change between viewing an offer on PackageHolidayCompare and visiting the supplier's website.
  • You should always check the final price, holiday details, baggage, room information and booking conditions on the supplier's website before completing a purchase.
  • The suppliers and offers shown may not represent every holiday supplier or every package holiday available in the market.

How PackageHolidayCompare may earn money

Some links on PackageHolidayCompare may be affiliate links. This means we may receive a commission from a supplier if you visit their website through one of our links and make a qualifying booking.

This does not increase the price you pay for your holiday.

Our comparison pages clearly identify supplier offers and direct customers to the relevant supplier website to complete the booking.
