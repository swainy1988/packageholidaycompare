Turkey offers a wide range of package holiday destinations, from large All Inclusive resorts around Antalya to beach resorts served through Dalaman and Bodrum. Comparing several areas, hotels and departure dates can help you find the holiday that best matches your budget and travel plans.

Turkey destinations to compare

Different parts of Turkey offer different styles of package holiday. It can be worth comparing more than one resort area before choosing your hotel.

Antalya

Antalya is a major gateway for many Mediterranean resorts, including areas with large All Inclusive hotels, family resorts and beaches.

Lara Beach

Lara Beach is known for large resort hotels and is popular with travellers looking for an All Inclusive, hotel-focused holiday.

Belek

Belek has a range of larger resorts and family-friendly hotels, with facilities that can include pools, entertainment and waterparks.

Side

Side combines resort hotels and beaches with access to historic surroundings, making it another area worth comparing around the Antalya region.

Dalaman Coast

Dalaman is a gateway for several popular resorts in south-west Turkey, including Marmaris, Fethiye and nearby coastal areas.

Bodrum

Bodrum and the surrounding peninsula offer beach resorts, hotels and a different atmosphere from Turkey's larger Antalya resort areas.

All Inclusive Turkey holidays

All Inclusive is widely available across Turkey's main resort areas. Depending on the hotel, packages may include selected meals, drinks, snacks and entertainment.

The exact inclusions can vary significantly, so compare the hotel description and supplier details before deciding which package offers the best value for your trip.

Antalya or Dalaman?

Antalya and Dalaman serve different collections of Turkish resorts. Antalya is often associated with large resort areas such as Lara Beach, Belek and Side, while Dalaman provides access to resorts including Marmaris and Fethiye.

There is no single best area for every traveller. Compare hotel style, transfer time, beaches and resort atmosphere before deciding.

When to visit Turkey

Turkey is particularly popular for package holidays during the warmer months, while availability and resort facilities can vary outside the main holiday season.

Weather is never guaranteed. Check the expected conditions and seasonal hotel facilities for your exact destination and travel dates before booking.

Tips for comparing Turkey holidays

1
Compare several resort areas

Antalya, Dalaman and Bodrum can offer different hotel styles, beaches and resort experiences.

2
Check the total package price

Compare the full holiday cost for everyone travelling rather than only a per-person figure.

3
Look at transfer times

Some resorts can be a significant distance from the arrival airport, which may matter when travelling with children.

4
Compare family facilities

Check pools, slides, kids' clubs, entertainment and room options before choosing a family resort.

5
Check All Inclusive details

Food, drinks, restaurants and entertainment included can vary between different hotels.

6
Confirm the final supplier price

Prices and availability can change, so always verify the final package details directly with the supplier.
