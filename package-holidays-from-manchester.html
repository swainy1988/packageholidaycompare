Finding a package holiday from Manchester

Manchester is a convenient departure point for travellers across the North West and surrounding areas. Package holidays can combine flights and accommodation into one booking, while some offers may also include transfers, baggage or meals. Comparing the total package rather than only the headline price can make it easier to understand what you are actually getting for your budget.

How to compare package holiday prices

A cheaper headline price does not always mean the best overall option. Compare what is included and make sure you are looking at the total cost for your travelling party.

1
Compare the total holiday price

Check whether the displayed amount is for the whole booking or per person. PackageHolidayCompare focuses on the total package budget used in your search.

2
Check the board basis

Room Only, Self Catering, Bed & Breakfast, Half Board and All Inclusive holidays can have very different overall costs once spending at the destination is considered.

3
Be flexible with dates

If your travel dates can move slightly, comparing nearby departures may give you more options.

4
Check the final supplier price

Holiday availability and prices can change. Always confirm the final amount, inclusions and booking terms on the supplier's website before paying.

Comparing family holidays from Manchester

Families may want to compare more than the hotel star rating. Room capacity, children's ages, meal arrangements, pools, entertainment and the journey from the destination airport can all affect how suitable a holiday feels.

When searching PackageHolidayCompare, enter the number of adults and children and add each child's age. This keeps the search information more useful as the range of available supplier offers grows.

When should you travel?

The best travel period depends on what you want from the holiday. Summer school holiday dates are popular with families, while travellers with more flexibility may compare spring, autumn or winter dates as well.

Instead of choosing a destination first, another approach is to start with the month you want to travel and compare several destinations that fit your budget and holiday style.
