💷 Affiliate Disclosure

How We May Earn Commission

PackageHolidayCompare is free for travellers to use. We may earn commission when users follow certain supplier links and make a qualifying booking.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Our affiliate disclosure

Some links from PackageHolidayCompare to holiday suppliers may be affiliate links.

This means that if you click a supplier link and later complete a qualifying booking, PackageHolidayCompare may receive a commission from the supplier or an affiliate network.

You do not pay PackageHolidayCompare for using our comparison service.

Receiving a commission does not increase the price you pay to PackageHolidayCompare. You should always check the final price shown by the supplier before completing a booking.

Why we use affiliate links

Running a holiday comparison website involves costs including website hosting, database services, development, security and ongoing maintenance.

Affiliate commissions can help support the operation and development of PackageHolidayCompare while allowing travellers to use the comparison service without paying us a search or comparison fee.

How supplier links work

When you find a holiday offer on PackageHolidayCompare, you may be given a link to continue to the holiday supplier.

When you select that link:

  • you leave PackageHolidayCompare;
  • you visit the supplier or relevant booking website;
  • you should check the latest price, availability and holiday details;
  • any booking is completed with the supplier rather than with PackageHolidayCompare;
  • we may receive a commission if the booking qualifies under our commercial arrangement with that supplier or affiliate network.

Affiliate notices on our comparison pages

Where supplier booking links are displayed, PackageHolidayCompare may show an affiliate disclosure near those links.

Example disclosure:

We may earn a commission if you book through a supplier link. This does not change the price you pay.

Supplier relationships

PackageHolidayCompare may have commercial or affiliate relationships with some of the suppliers displayed on the website.

We may not have a commercial relationship with every holiday supplier in the market, and not every supplier or package holiday will necessarily be available through our comparison service.

The suppliers available for comparison may therefore change as new commercial relationships, affiliate programmes or data arrangements are introduced.

Holiday prices

Prices displayed on PackageHolidayCompare are intended to help travellers compare available offers.

Holiday prices and availability can change, sometimes quickly.

PackageHolidayCompare does not take payment for the holiday and cannot guarantee that a price displayed on our website will still be available when you visit the supplier.

The final price displayed by the supplier before you complete your booking should always be checked carefully.

Bookings are made with the supplier

PackageHolidayCompare acts as a comparison service.

We do not become a party to the holiday booking contract simply because you found an offer through our website.

Your booking, payment and contractual relationship are with the holiday supplier you choose.

The supplier's own terms, booking conditions, cancellation policies and privacy policies will apply.

Click and conversion reporting

PackageHolidayCompare may record when a supplier deal link is selected so that we can understand how our comparison service and supplier offers are being used.

We may also receive or record information confirming that a qualifying booking or conversion has taken place.

This information can be used for affiliate reporting, supplier performance measurement and calculating commission.

More information about data handling is available in our Privacy Policy .

External supplier websites

Once you follow a supplier link, you are visiting a website operated by another organisation.

PackageHolidayCompare does not control how that external website operates or the technologies it uses.

The supplier or affiliate network may use its own cookies or tracking technologies. Their own privacy, cookie and booking terms will apply.

Changes to affiliate relationships

Our commercial relationships may change over time.

We may add or remove holiday suppliers, affiliate networks or other commercial partners as PackageHolidayCompare develops.

We may update this disclosure to reflect material changes to how our affiliate relationships operate.
