Comparing family holidays from Manchester

Finding a suitable family holiday involves more than comparing the headline price. Room layout, children's ages, board basis, pools, entertainment, transfer time and the overall hotel environment can all affect whether a package works well for your family.

What should families compare?

Different families have different priorities, so it helps to think about the facilities and arrangements that matter most before choosing a hotel.

🛏️

Family rooms

Check the room capacity, bed layout and whether children share the same sleeping area or have a separate space.

🏊

Pools and activities

Swimming pools, slides, sports and daytime activities can be important when choosing a resort for children.

🍽️

Food and board basis

Consider whether Self Catering, Bed & Breakfast, Half Board or All Inclusive is most suitable for your family's plans.

Family holiday destinations to compare

Your preferred destination may depend on the time of year, your budget and the type of resort your family enjoys.

Spain

Spain offers a wide range of beach resorts, islands and family-focused hotels with different board options.

Turkey

Turkey has many larger resort hotels with pools, entertainment, children's facilities and All Inclusive options.

Egypt

Red Sea resorts can appeal to families looking for large hotels, beaches, pools and waterpark-style facilities.

Greece

Greece provides a mixture of island and mainland resorts, from larger family hotels to quieter coastal locations.

Cyprus

Cyprus can suit families looking for Mediterranean beach holidays with a variety of resort and hotel styles.

Portugal

The Algarve and other Portuguese holiday areas provide family resorts, beaches and a range of accommodation options.

Why children's ages matter

Holiday suppliers may use children's ages when calculating package prices and determining room occupancy. That means entering the correct age for each child is important when comparing family holiday options.

PackageHolidayCompare allows you to enter the number of children travelling and their ages when you perform a search.

All Inclusive or another board basis?

All Inclusive can make budgeting easier because selected meals and drinks may already form part of the package price. This can be attractive for families spending a lot of time at their resort.

Other families may prefer Self Catering or Bed & Breakfast if they plan to explore the destination and eat away from the hotel.

Family holiday checklist

Before choosing a package, check the details that could have the biggest impact on your family's trip.

✓
Check room occupancy

Make sure the room can accommodate everyone in your travelling party and check the sleeping arrangements.

✓
Check children's facilities

Look at pools, kids' clubs, entertainment, play areas and any age restrictions that may apply.

✓
Consider the transfer

A longer airport-to-hotel transfer may matter more when travelling with younger children.

✓
Compare the total family price

Look at the total package cost for the full travelling party rather than only a per-person headline price.

✓
Check baggage and extras

Review baggage allowances, transfers and other extras before deciding which package offers the most suitable overall value.

✓
Confirm the final details

Holiday prices and availability can change. Always verify the final information with the supplier before completing a booking.

Travelling during school holidays

School holiday periods are popular times for family travel. If your dates are fixed, comparing several destinations and hotels can give you a broader range of options.

Families with more flexibility may also want to compare nearby departure dates, as the available hotels and package prices can vary.
