October Sun Holidays from Manchester

Looking for warmer weather as autumn arrives in the UK? Explore package holiday destinations to consider for an October getaway from Manchester.

Read the guide

Where can you find October sun?

October can be a popular time to escape cooler UK weather. Some Mediterranean destinations may still offer pleasant conditions, while destinations further south can provide stronger warm-weather options. Weather is never guaranteed, so it is useful to compare several destinations before choosing your holiday.

Why consider an October holiday?

October can suit travellers looking for an autumn break after the main summer season. Depending on your travel dates and destination, you may find a different range of hotels and package options compared with July and August.

October sun destinations to compare

Conditions vary by destination and year, so check forecasts closer to travel. These destinations are useful starting points when comparing autumn package holidays.

Egypt

Red Sea resort areas such as Hurghada can appeal to travellers looking for beach resorts, pools, waterparks and All Inclusive hotel options later in the year.

Canary Islands

Islands such as Tenerife, Gran Canaria, Lanzarote and Fuerteventura are commonly considered for autumn and winter sunshine.

Cyprus

Cyprus can remain an option for travellers looking for a Mediterranean holiday into the autumn season.

Turkey

Southern Turkish resort areas may appeal to travellers considering a late-season package holiday, although conditions can become more variable as autumn progresses.

Morocco

Morocco can offer a different style of autumn holiday, including resort and city-based options depending on where you choose to stay.

Spain

Spanish destinations can still be worth comparing in October, particularly the Canary Islands and other southern options.

October family holidays from Manchester

Families travelling during school holiday periods may want to compare several destinations rather than focusing on only one resort.

Hotel pools, children's clubs, entertainment, room layouts and transfer times can all affect whether a package suits your family.

All Inclusive October holidays

All Inclusive can be useful for travellers who want selected meals, drinks and resort facilities included within the overall package.

In resort-focused destinations, this can make it easier to understand much of the holiday cost before travelling. Always check exactly what the individual hotel's All Inclusive package contains.

Tips for comparing October holidays

1
Compare several destinations

Weather and hotel availability can differ significantly between destinations during autumn.

2
Check your exact travel dates

Package availability and prices may change around popular school holiday periods.

3
Look at the total package price

Compare the full cost for your travelling party rather than only the headline per-person amount.

4
Check what is included

Review baggage, board basis, transfers, room type and other important package details before choosing.

5
Check the weather nearer departure

Historical conditions can help with planning, but weather varies and cannot be guaranteed for a particular holiday.

6
Confirm the final supplier price

Holiday prices and availability can change, so verify the final booking details on the supplier's website.
