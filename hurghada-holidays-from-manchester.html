Planning a Hurghada holiday from Manchester

Hurghada is one of Egypt's best-known Red Sea resort areas. Travellers can compare a wide range of hotels, including family resorts, beachfront properties, waterpark hotels and All Inclusive package holidays.

Why choose Hurghada?

Hurghada can suit travellers looking for a resort-focused holiday with pools, beaches, restaurants and entertainment. The area has a wide range of hotel styles, so comparing several resorts can be worthwhile.

🏖️

Red Sea resorts

Hurghada has a large selection of resorts with access to beaches, pools and hotel facilities.

🍹

All Inclusive options

Many Hurghada hotels offer All Inclusive board, making the destination popular for resort-based holidays.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family hotels

Families can compare resorts with waterparks, kids' clubs, entertainment, pools and larger room options.

What to compare when choosing a Hurghada resort

Hurghada holidays can vary significantly depending on the hotel, room type, board basis and resort facilities included in your package.

Waterparks and pools

If water facilities are important, compare the number of pools, slides, children's areas and any restrictions.

Beach access

Some travellers prefer a beachfront resort while others are happy to stay slightly further inland for different hotel facilities.

Room type

Compare standard rooms, family rooms, suites and any swim-up room options available through the supplier.

Transfer time

Check how far the hotel is from the airport, particularly if travelling with younger children.

All Inclusive details

Check which restaurants, drinks, snacks and hotel facilities are included in the package.

Family facilities

Look for children's entertainment, kids' clubs, family rooms and activities that suit your particular family.

Family holidays to Hurghada

Hurghada has many large resort hotels that can appeal to families. When comparing family holidays, consider the children's facilities, hotel layout, room size and transfer time as well as the overall package price.

Waterpark hotels can be particularly attractive to families, but always check which slides and facilities are suitable for the ages of the children travelling.

All Inclusive Hurghada holidays

All Inclusive is common among resort-focused holidays in Hurghada. Depending on the hotel, the package may include selected meals, drinks, snacks and entertainment.

Always check the exact hotel and supplier description because the meaning of All Inclusive can differ between resorts.

When to visit Hurghada

Hurghada attracts holidaymakers at different times of year. Some visitors choose the summer months, while others consider Egypt for autumn, winter or spring sunshine.

Temperatures and conditions vary throughout the year and weather is never guaranteed. Check expected conditions for your actual travel dates before booking.

Tips for comparing Hurghada holidays

1
Compare the total holiday cost

Look at the complete package price for everyone travelling, rather than only the per-person amount.

2
Check room occupancy

Families should check how many proper beds, sofa beds or extra beds are included in the selected room.

3
Compare hotel facilities

A slightly different resort may offer better pools, restaurants or facilities for your family.

4
Check baggage and transfers

Confirm whether checked baggage and airport transfers are included in the package you are comparing.

5
Compare nearby departure dates

If your dates are flexible, compare nearby departures because available hotels and package prices can change.

6
Confirm final supplier details

Always verify the final price, availability and package inclusions on the supplier's website.
