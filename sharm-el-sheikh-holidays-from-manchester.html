Planning a Sharm El Sheikh holiday from Manchester

Sharm El Sheikh is one of Egypt's best-known Red Sea resort destinations. Travellers can compare a range of resort hotels, including All Inclusive properties, family resorts, beachfront hotels and hotels with pools, entertainment and leisure facilities.

Why choose Sharm El Sheikh?

Sharm El Sheikh can appeal to travellers who want a resort-focused holiday with sunshine, beaches, swimming pools and hotel facilities. Different resort areas offer different styles of holiday, so comparing several hotels can help you find the right option.

Sharm El Sheikh areas to compare

The wider Sharm El Sheikh resort area includes several different locations. Hotel facilities and atmosphere can vary, so check where your chosen resort is located before booking.

Naama Bay

Naama Bay is one of the better-known areas around Sharm El Sheikh and offers hotels, restaurants and leisure options.

Nabq Bay

Nabq Bay has a selection of large resort hotels and can appeal to travellers looking for a hotel-focused holiday.

Sharks Bay

Sharks Bay is another resort area where travellers can compare beachfront hotels and Red Sea holiday options.

Ras Um Sid

Ras Um Sid is worth comparing if location, coastline and resort facilities are important to your holiday plans.

Sharm El Sheikh or Hurghada?

Sharm El Sheikh and Hurghada are both major Red Sea holiday destinations, but the available hotels, resort areas and facilities can differ. If you are flexible about where in Egypt you stay, comparing both destinations can give you more package holiday options.

All Inclusive Sharm El Sheikh holidays

All Inclusive can be convenient for travellers who expect to spend a large amount of their holiday at the resort.

Packages may include selected meals, drinks, snacks and entertainment, but inclusions vary between hotels. Always check the exact supplier and hotel description before choosing a deal.

Family holidays to Sharm El Sheikh

Families can compare hotels based on swimming pools, children's facilities, entertainment, kids' clubs and available room types.

When comparing family packages, also consider the total holiday price, baggage allowance, transfers and whether the room is suitable for everyone travelling.

When to visit Sharm El Sheikh

Travellers consider Sharm El Sheikh at different times throughout the year. Some choose Egypt for summer holidays while others compare it for autumn, winter or spring trips.

Temperatures and conditions change throughout the year and weather is never guaranteed. Check expected conditions for your exact travel dates before booking.
