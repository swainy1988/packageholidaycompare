Planning an Egypt holiday from Manchester

Egypt is a popular destination for travellers looking for resort holidays, sunshine, beaches, swimming pools and All Inclusive hotels. Comparing different resorts, hotels and travel dates can help you find the package that best matches your family, budget and holiday plans.

Why compare Egypt package holidays?

Egypt offers a wide range of resort styles, from large family hotels with entertainment and waterparks to quieter beach resorts. Package inclusions vary, so it is important to compare the complete holiday rather than only the headline price.

Egypt holiday destinations to compare

Egypt has several different resort areas. The right choice depends on the type of hotel, beach, activities and atmosphere you want from your holiday.

Hurghada

Hurghada is a major Red Sea resort area with a large selection of hotels, beaches, swimming pools and All Inclusive resorts.

Sharm El Sheikh

Sharm El Sheikh offers resort hotels, beaches and access to the Red Sea. It can appeal to couples, families and travellers looking for a resort-focused holiday.

Marsa Alam

Marsa Alam can suit travellers looking for a more resort-based Red Sea break, with many holidays centred around the hotel and coastline.

Makadi Bay

Makadi Bay is a resort area near Hurghada with hotels that can be particularly attractive to families and All Inclusive holidaymakers.

Soma Bay

Soma Bay is another Red Sea resort area worth comparing if you are looking for beach hotels and a relaxing resort stay.

El Gouna

El Gouna offers a different resort atmosphere with hotels, lagoons, restaurants and leisure options.

All Inclusive Egypt holidays

All Inclusive is a popular board option for resort-based holidays because selected meals, drinks and facilities may already be included.

Before choosing an offer, check exactly what the hotel's All Inclusive package covers. Different hotels and suppliers can have different rules, meal times, drink selections and included facilities.

Family holidays to Egypt

Families can compare hotels based on facilities such as children's pools, waterparks, kids' clubs, entertainment and family-sized rooms.

It is also worth comparing airport transfers, room type, board basis and the total price for everyone travelling.

When should you visit Egypt?

Conditions in Egypt can vary throughout the year. Some travellers choose Egypt for summer holidays, while others look at the destination for autumn, winter or spring sunshine.

Weather is never guaranteed, so check expected conditions for your exact destination and travel dates before booking.

Tips for comparing Egypt holidays

1
Compare more than one resort

Hurghada, Sharm El Sheikh and other Red Sea areas can offer different hotels and holiday experiences.

2
Check the total package price

Compare the total holiday cost for everyone travelling rather than relying only on a per-person figure.

3
Look at transfer times

The distance between the airport and hotel can be important, particularly when travelling with children.

4
Compare room types

Check whether the package includes a standard room, family room, swim-up room or another room type.

5
Check what All Inclusive means

Included food, drinks and facilities can vary between different hotels.

6
Confirm current travel information

Before travelling, check current entry requirements, travel advice and supplier information relevant to your trip.
