Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how PackageHolidayCompare handles information when you use our holiday comparison website or contact us.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. About this policy

PackageHolidayCompare operates a website that allows users to search and compare package holiday offers from participating holiday suppliers.

This privacy policy explains what information may be handled when you use our website, why it may be used and the choices available to you.

If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at:

support@packageholidaycompare.co.uk

2. Information you provide to us

You may provide information to us when you contact PackageHolidayCompare by email.

This may include information such as:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • the contents of your message;
  • information about a holiday offer or supplier you are contacting us about;
  • any other information you choose to include in your communication.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for us to deal with your enquiry.

3. Holiday search information

When you use the PackageHolidayCompare search, you may enter information such as your departure airport, destination, travel dates, number of travellers, board basis and budget.

Search information may be stored locally in your browser so that your search can continue between the search results and hotel pages.

This type of browser storage is used to provide the comparison functionality and does not by itself identify you by name.

4. Supplier link and click information

When you select a supplier deal link on PackageHolidayCompare, we may record information about that click so we can understand how our comparison service is being used.

The information recorded may include:

  • the holiday offer selected;
  • the hotel connected with that offer;
  • the supplier;
  • whether the click came from a results page or hotel page;
  • the date and time of the click.

Our comparison click records are designed for reporting and supplier performance purposes and do not require your name or email address.

5. Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting, database or security providers may process technical information required to operate and protect the website.

This may include information such as browser type, device information, IP address, access times, error information and security logs.

This information may be used to keep the website secure, diagnose technical problems and maintain the service.

6. How we use information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • operating and maintaining PackageHolidayCompare;
  • providing holiday search and comparison functionality;
  • responding to customer, supplier and partnership enquiries;
  • understanding how users interact with supplier offers;
  • monitoring website performance and security;
  • investigating incorrect or outdated information;
  • meeting legal or regulatory obligations where applicable.

7. Legal bases

Where UK data protection law applies, personal information must be processed using an appropriate legal basis.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Legitimate interests – where processing is reasonably necessary to operate, improve, secure or understand the use of our comparison service.
  • Consent – where consent is required and you have chosen to provide it.
  • Legal obligation – where information must be processed to comply with a legal requirement.
  • Contract or steps requested by you – where relevant to a specific service or commercial enquiry.

8. Holiday suppliers and external websites

PackageHolidayCompare is a comparison service. Holiday bookings and payments are completed on the website of the holiday supplier you choose.

When you follow a supplier link, you leave PackageHolidayCompare and the supplier's own privacy policy and terms will apply.

We recommend reading the relevant supplier's privacy information before providing personal information or completing a booking.

9. Service providers

We may use third-party service providers to help operate PackageHolidayCompare, such as website hosting, database, email and security providers.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services, subject to their own contractual and legal obligations.

10. Affiliate relationships

Some supplier links may be affiliate links. If you follow one of these links and complete a qualifying booking, PackageHolidayCompare may receive a commission.

Affiliate or supplier partners may use their own tracking technologies after you visit their website. Their own privacy and cookie policies will apply to that activity.

11. How long information is kept

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including where information is required for security, record keeping or legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and why it is held.

12. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • request access to personal information held about you;
  • ask for inaccurate information to be corrected;
  • request deletion of information in certain circumstances;
  • request restriction of processing in certain circumstances;
  • object to certain types of processing;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • request data portability where the right applies.

To make a privacy request, contact:

support@packageholidaycompare.co.uk

13. Complaints

If you have a concern about how your information has been handled, please contact us first so we have an opportunity to investigate.

You may also have the right to make a complaint to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

14. Children

PackageHolidayCompare allows adults to enter the number and ages of children travelling as part of a holiday search.

The service is not designed for children to create accounts or provide personal contact information directly to us.

15. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information and restrict unauthorised access.

However, no internet service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

16. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy as PackageHolidayCompare develops, new suppliers or services are added, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact us about privacy

For privacy or data protection questions, requests or concerns, contact:

support@packageholidaycompare.co.uk
