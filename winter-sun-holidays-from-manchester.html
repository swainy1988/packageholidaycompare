Planning a winter sun holiday from Manchester

Winter sun can mean different things depending on the destination. Some travellers want warm beach weather, while others simply want a milder and brighter break away from the UK winter. Comparing several destinations can help you find the type of holiday that best matches your plans.

Why take a winter sun holiday?

A winter package holiday can provide a break from shorter days and colder weather at home. The right destination depends on how warm you want it to be, your budget and how far you are prepared to travel.

Winter sun destinations to compare

Weather varies by month and destination and cannot be guaranteed. These are useful places to consider when starting a winter holiday search.

Canary Islands

Tenerife, Gran Canaria, Lanzarote and Fuerteventura are popular options for travellers looking for a winter break without travelling long haul.

Egypt

Red Sea resort areas such as Hurghada can be worth comparing for resort holidays, beaches, pools and All Inclusive hotels.

Morocco

Morocco can offer a mixture of resort, beach and city holidays for travellers wanting a different winter getaway.

Southern Spain

Southern Spanish destinations may provide a milder winter break, although conditions can be more changeable than destinations further south.

Tunisia

Tunisia can be worth comparing for resort-focused package holidays, although winter conditions may be cooler than peak summer months.

Cyprus

Cyprus can offer a quieter Mediterranean winter break, but travellers looking for hotter beach weather may want to compare it with destinations further south.

December, January or February?

The best destination can change depending on when you travel. December can include popular Christmas and New Year departures, while January and February can appeal to travellers looking for a break later in winter.

If your dates are flexible, compare several weeks rather than assuming one particular departure date will offer the best combination of hotel, destination and package price.

All Inclusive winter sun holidays

All Inclusive can be particularly convenient for resort holidays because selected meals, drinks and facilities may already form part of the package.

In destinations where you plan to spend a large amount of time at the hotel, comparing the All Inclusive package as a whole can be more useful than looking only at the room price.

Winter sun holidays for families

Families may want to look for hotels with children's pools, entertainment, larger rooms, waterparks or kids' clubs.

Also consider whether outdoor pools are heated during winter and which hotel facilities operate during your particular travel dates.

Tips for comparing winter holidays

1
Decide how warm you want it

A mild winter break and a hot beach holiday are different searches, so compare destinations accordingly.

2
Compare nearby dates

Christmas, New Year and school holidays can affect the options available for your trip.

3
Check hotel winter facilities

Some facilities may operate differently outside the main summer season, so check the hotel details.

4
Compare the total package price

Look at the total cost for everyone travelling rather than only the headline per-person amount.

5
Check what is included

Review baggage, transfers, room type, board basis and other package details before choosing.

6
Confirm the final supplier details

Prices and availability can change. Always verify the final booking price and holiday details with the supplier.
