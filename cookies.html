🍪 Cookies & Browser Storage

Cookie Policy

This policy explains how cookies, browser storage and similar technologies may be used when you visit PackageHolidayCompare.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites can store on your device when you visit them.

Cookies can be used for purposes such as remembering preferences, keeping a website secure, understanding how a website is used and supporting advertising or affiliate services.

Websites can also use other browser technologies such as local storage. Local storage allows information to be saved in your browser without using a traditional cookie.

2. Browser storage used by our holiday search

PackageHolidayCompare currently uses browser local storage to remember information entered into the holiday search.

This helps us carry your search between pages, such as from the homepage to the search results and then to a hotel page.

Information stored may include:

  • departure airport;
  • holiday destination;
  • departure and return dates;
  • number of nights;
  • number of adults and children;
  • board basis;
  • maximum holiday budget.

This information is used to provide the comparison functionality and does not require your name or email address.

3. Technologies currently used

Type Purpose Category
Browser local storage Remembers holiday search criteria while you move between search results and hotel pages. Functional
Website hosting and security technologies Our hosting, database or security providers may use technical mechanisms required to deliver and protect the website. Strictly necessary / security

4. Supplier link tracking

PackageHolidayCompare records certain information when a user selects a holiday supplier deal link.

This allows us to understand which offers and suppliers are receiving interest through our comparison service.

Our own deal-click records may include the supplier, holiday offer, hotel, source page and time of the click.

This tracking does not require us to place an advertising cookie on your device.

5. Affiliate links

Some links on PackageHolidayCompare may be affiliate links.

This means that if you follow a supplier link and later complete a qualifying booking, we may receive a commission.

When you leave PackageHolidayCompare and visit a supplier, affiliate network or other external website, that third party may use its own cookies or tracking technologies.

Those technologies are controlled by the relevant third party and are subject to that organisation's own privacy and cookie policies.

6. Analytics and advertising cookies

PackageHolidayCompare does not currently intentionally set non-essential third-party advertising or behavioural advertising cookies through the public website.

If we introduce analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookie technologies in the future, we will update this policy and, where required by law, provide appropriate cookie controls or consent choices.

7. Managing browser storage

Most browsers allow you to remove cookies and website storage using the browser's privacy or site-data settings.

Removing PackageHolidayCompare browser storage may clear your saved holiday search information, meaning you may need to enter your search again.

You can also configure your browser to block or restrict certain types of cookies. Blocking technologies that are necessary for a website to operate may affect how some website features work.

8. Third-party websites

PackageHolidayCompare contains links to holiday suppliers and may contain links to other external services.

Once you follow an external link, you are using that third party's website and its own cookie and privacy policies will apply.

PackageHolidayCompare does not control the cookies or browser technologies placed by third-party websites after you leave our service.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this cookie policy when the technologies used by PackageHolidayCompare change or when new suppliers, affiliate services, analytics tools or other services are introduced.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
